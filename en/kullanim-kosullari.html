Service

Ciro is a cash-tracking app designed for tradespeople and small businesses. It works offline on your device without connecting an account. The free plan covers one ledger and up to 30 accounts. More than 30 accounts, adding photos to transactions, sending and printing Excel and PDF, the 12-week cash flow, receipt reading, additional business ledgers and partner/staff invitations depend on the plans; the Ciro Premium screen in the app lists which feature belongs to which plan. The content of the plans and the limits of the free plan may change over time; the current version is published on this page.

Free promotional periods may be offered from time to time. During such a period, plan features are open to everyone and no payment is taken. The records you enter belong to you even when your plan changes. When your subscription ends, the ledgers and features outside the free plan are locked: one ledger stays open; your other ledgers remain on your device and open again when you subscribe again.

Subscriptions

  • Plans and duration: Ciro Premium (10 business ledgers, 10 partners or staff) is offered as a monthly or yearly subscription. Existing Pro and Team subscriptions continue as Ciro Premium. The current price and currency are shown at purchase according to your store account's country. A yearly plan is charged once per year.
  • Free trials: Plans may include a free trial. Eligibility is granted once per store account. If you do not cancel before the trial ends, the subscription is charged for the selected period.
  • Automatic renewal: Unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the period, the subscription renews automatically for the same duration and is charged to your store account.
  • Cancellation: You can cancel at any time through the App Store (Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions) or Google Play (Account → Payments and subscriptions). Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period; you retain access for the remaining time. Deleting your in-app account does not cancel the subscription.
  • Refunds: Payments are collected by the store. Refund requests are subject to that store's refund policy.
  • Plan changes: Plans are in the same subscription group. Upgrades and downgrades follow the store's transition rules.
  • Partners and staff: The business owner pays for the plan. Invited partners or staff do not pay separately for this sharing.
