Ciro (Simple Money Management) Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Summary: Basic bookkeeping works offline without connecting an account. In this mode, ledger records and receipt photos do not leave the device. Google ML Kit, used for Android receipt scanning, may process technical diagnostic and usage data. If you connect an optional cloud account, your email address and a ledger backup encrypted on your device are processed. If you use partner, staff or contact account sharing, the shared records are stored on the server encrypted in transit and at rest and are shown only to the people you authorize; this is not end-to-end encryption. Notifications are delivered through Google and Apple servers and do not include amounts. If you purchase a paid subscription, the store's transaction identifier is linked to your account. No advertising SDK is used.

Data collected and purposes

Without an account, financial records, settings and receipt photos are stored only on your device. Receipt text recognition takes place on the device.

Android receipt scanning: Google ML Kit may send technical information to Google over HTTPS for diagnostics and usage analysis, including device model and operating system, app version, per-installation identifier, performance measurements, API configuration, event types and error codes. The receipt image and recognized text are processed on the device. This technical data processing does not depend on connecting a cloud account. See the Google ML Kit data disclosure and Google Privacy Policy. This Android disclosure does not change on-device receipt recognition on iOS.

If an optional account is connected, the following data may be processed:

  • Email address: account creation, sign-in verification and, in contact account sharing, showing the sharer who accepted the link.
  • Account identifier and invitation label (name or nickname): managing access rights, identifying the invited person and showing who entered a transaction.
  • Ledger backup encrypted on the device using AES-256-GCM: backup and restoration on a new device.
  • If a staff invitation is used, the records staff send (such as type, amount, date, description and selected account), their approval status, rejection reason and who decided: delivering the records to the business owner for approval and showing staff the record status, daily summary or contact list according to the selected permissions. See “Staff” below.
  • If a partner invitation is used, the shared ledger's records and who entered or last changed each record: keeping the ledger together with the partner. See “Shared ledgers” below.
  • If contact account sharing is used, the contact account name and the sharer's name, the recipient's name and email address, the selected contact account's transactions and the entries the recipient sends: showing the selected contact account to the paired person and passing their entries to the sharer for approval. See “Contact account sharing” below.
  • If notification permission is given, the device's notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging), platform (Android/iOS), app language and app version: sending news of new entries, approvals and invitations in shared ledgers to your phone. At most 10 devices are kept per account; a token that becomes invalid is deleted.
  • If a paid App Store / Google Play subscription is purchased, the store-provided transaction identifier, plan name, expiry date, store name and store environment (production/sandbox): verifying that the subscription belongs to your account, enabling cloud and invitation entitlements and preventing transfer of the same subscription to another account. Your account identifier is also sent to the store during purchase: appAccountToken to the App Store, or its one-way SHA-256 hash to Google Play. Payment details, such as card data, remain with the store and are not provided to the app. Subscription purchases, renewals, cancellations and refunds are handled through our subscription infrastructure provider RevenueCat (USA); for this, this information is shared with RevenueCat, together with your account identifier if you are signed in, and RevenueCat also processes technical details such as device model, operating system and app version.
  • Daily account download count and total bytes: limiting backup download abuse.
  • Backup download IP quota: without writing the raw IP address to the app database, a one-way SHA-256 hash is generated using a server-only secret. The hash is processed with the daily download count and total bytes.
  • The infrastructure provider's security and access logs: standard connection information such as IP address and timestamp.

The cloud backup is encrypted on your phone and stored encrypted on the server. The encryption key is protected in connection with your account; this is what lets you sign in on a new phone and restore your backup. This method is not end-to-end encryption.

How shared data is stored

Shared ledgers, staff records and contact account sharing are stored on the server, in a database in Supabase's Frankfurt (EU) region. They are protected with HTTPS in transit and with the provider's disk encryption at rest. This data is not end-to-end encrypted. Access is limited by database row permissions, and only the authorized parties listed below can see it. The provider takes backups of the database to guard against data loss; these backups are kept with the same protection. Personal ledgers that you do not share and the encrypted cloud backup are not affected and remain as described above.

For each shared record, the user account ID of the person who entered or last changed it and the time are stored. Deleted records are not removed immediately; they stay with a “deleted” mark so that other devices can also see the deletion. In a shared ledger, the content of a deleted record is erased; in contact account sharing, the content of a deleted transaction is no longer sent to the other party. The encrypted shared ledger copy and staff record envelopes used by older app versions may remain on the server for a while until the transition is complete.

Shared ledgers

When a business owner invites a partner, the shared ledger's records (transactions, contact accounts and their transactions, accounts and balances, stock and settings) are stored on the server record by record. A partner with write access sees and writes to the whole ledger; a view-only partner can see it but cannot write. The owner's email address, account key and other unshared ledgers are not shown to the partner. Each device keeps a copy of the records so it can also work offline. If both parties change the same record differently, the user is asked which one should apply.

The owner can remove sharing in the app at any time. After removal, the partner loses access to the ledger on the server and receives no new changes. A copy already downloaded to the partner's device may remain there until they remove that ledger from their device.

Staff

Staff write the records they send (such as transaction type, amount, date, description and selected account) to an approval box on the server. This box is read by the devices the business owner is signed in on and by partners with write access; they approve records or reject them with a reason. Staff see only what they sent, its approval status and any rejection reason; they cannot see the rest of the ledger. Depending on the selected permissions, staff may be shown record status, a daily summary or the contact list. Staff can select the name and type of the owner's open accounts; total cash balance, account limits and bank details are not shared in this list. If the owner allows it, eligible records may be processed automatically on any open device with write access. An approved record is written to the ledger with the entry author's name, role and account ID.

Contact account sharing

Instead of the whole ledger, a single contact account can be shared with another Ciro user, such as your customer. Each share covers one contact account and one recipient. Sharing uses a link that is valid for 7 days. The link opens as a ready-made message in your own WhatsApp, and you confirm sending it. The message and phone number are passed to WhatsApp through a wa.me link; the phone number is not sent to Ciro's server. The secret code in the link sits after the “#” sign in the address and is not sent to the web server that serves the page. The link is part of the message you send; you decide who receives the message. Ciro's database stores only a SHA-256 hash of this code.

When the recipient signs in with a verified email, Google or Apple account and accepts, the share opens immediately; no separate approval by the sharer is needed. The first account to accept the link is bound to the share. Send the link only to the person you want to share with; if someone else accepts the link, you can close their access by removing the share. When the link is accepted, you are informed (as a notification if you allowed notifications), you see the recipient's name and email address, and you can remove the share with one tap. The recipient cannot see the sharer's email address.

The recipient is shown only the selected contact account's name, the sharer's name, that account's transactions (date, amount, direction and description), the balance calculated from them and technical identifiers. The account's private notes, phone, other contact accounts, and cash and bank accounts are not shared. While the share remains active, transactions added, changed or deleted in this contact account are also reflected to the recipient. If the “Can add entries” permission is given, the recipient can send a sale, purchase, collection or payment; it appears as “Awaiting approval” on both sides, does not enter the ledger until the sharer approves it, and if approved, is recorded with the sender's name and user account ID.

On the server, the contact account name, the sharer's name, the recipient's name and email address, user account IDs, ledger and contact account IDs, permission, transactions, their status and dates are protected as described in the section “How shared data is stored” above. Only the sharer can remove a share; after removal, the recipient's access closes immediately and no new transactions reach them. Information the recipient has already seen cannot be taken back. The recipient can ask the sharer to remove it, delete their account or write to cirodestek@gmail.com.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, events in shared ledgers (such as a partner adding an entry, staff sending a record, an approval or rejection, a contact account transaction, an invitation being accepted or a share being removed) are sent to your phone as notifications. For this, your device's notification token, platform, app language and app version are stored on the server linked to your account. Notifications are delivered through Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and, on iOS, also through the Apple Push Notification service; these services process the token and notification text to deliver the notification. Notification text does not include amounts; it contains short information such as a business or person name and the type of event. Pending notifications are kept on the server for at most 7 days and then deleted. You can turn off notification types in the app or withdraw notification permission in your device settings.

Transfers and storage

Optional cloud data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored in Supabase's Frankfurt, Germany region. If you choose Google sign-in, authentication takes place through Google; if you choose Apple sign-in, through Apple. The app receives only the identity token and email address, if provided, from these providers.

Shared data is stored in the same Supabase region. Notifications are delivered through Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging) and Apple (Apple Push Notification service) servers, which may be located outside Türkiye and the European Union. Transfers of personal data abroad are subject to the transfer conditions in Article 9 of KVKK.

The service providers to which data is transferred are: Supabase (database and file storage; Frankfurt, Germany), Google (Sign in with Google and delivery of notifications), Apple (Sign in with Apple and delivery of notifications) and RevenueCat (subscription operations; USA). These providers process the data only to provide the listed services.

Daily exchange and gold rates are fetched from a public rates service (finans.truncgil.com). No account or ledger data is sent with this request; as with any internet request, your IP address reaches the service.

Retention and deletion

Cloud data is retained while the account remains open. The daily account download counter resets on the first download of a new UTC day and is removed when the account is deleted. IP-hash quota rows, which have no user account identifier field and are generated using a server-only secret, become eligible for cleanup after two days and are automatically deleted in small batches during subsequent downloads. The infrastructure provider's standard security logs follow its own security and retention rules.

Shared data: While a share continues, records, deletion marks and the rows needed for change history remain on the server. When a share is removed, the other party's access closes immediately. If you delete your account, the shared ledgers and contact account shares you own are deleted from the server; partners, staff and recipients lose access, although copies already downloaded to their own devices may remain there. Records you entered as a member in someone else's ledger stay in that ledger; the server's “entered by” field (your account ID) on those records is cleared, while a name and role shown inside a record may remain as long as the record stays in the ledger. Records you sent as staff that have not yet been processed stay in the business's approval box; your account ID on the server is deleted at the latest when the record is processed. Those already approved and written to the ledger stay in the ledger. If the recipient of a contact account share deletes their account, the record and history of the business that opened the share remain; the recipient's name and email address on the server are deleted. Your notification tokens and preferences are deleted too.

You can permanently delete your account, email, cloud backups, sharing data, account-linked subscription records and download counter in the app through Menu → Backup and data → Cloud backup → Permanently delete account. Deleting the account does not cancel the store subscription; cancel it separately through your store account. If you used Sign in with Apple, also remove Ciro under Apple Account → Sign-In & Security → Sign in with Apple; this link is not automatically removed when the account is deleted. See the account deletion page for detailed steps.

Children's privacy

The app is designed for business owners, is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Rights and contact

Under Article 11 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK), you have the right to learn whether your personal data is processed; request information if it is; learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used in line with that purpose; know the third parties to whom it is transferred in Türkiye or abroad; request correction if it is incomplete or inaccurate; request its deletion or destruction; request that these operations be notified to the parties to whom the data was transferred; object to an outcome against you that results from analysis made solely by automated systems; and request compensation if you suffer damage due to unlawful processing.

You can exercise your right to deletion immediately through Menu → Backup and data → Cloud backup → Permanently delete account. For access, correction or deletion requests when you cannot access the app, contact cirodestek@gmail.com. Requests are answered within the period prescribed by KVKK, no later than 30 days.

How data is collected and the legal basis

Data is collected from the information you enter while using the app, from the sign-in method you choose when you connect an account (email, Google or Apple) and automatically from your device. Data needed for the account, backup, sharing and subscription is processed to perform the service relationship established with you (KVKK Art. 5/2-c); security records and counters that prevent abuse are processed under the legitimate interest of keeping the service secure (KVKK Art. 5/2-f); notifications are processed on the basis of the permission you give. Connecting an account and using sharing are not mandatory; the app also works without an account.

Data security

To protect your data, encryption in transit and at rest, per-account access permissions and access records are used.

If you live in the European Economic Area, your rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) also apply; if you live in Brazil, your rights under the General Data Protection Law (LGPD) also apply: accessing your data, correcting it, deleting it, restricting processing, objecting to processing, receiving your data in a portable form and withdrawing consent you have given.
